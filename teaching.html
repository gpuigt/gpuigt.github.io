Teaching


Graduate and engineering schools

  • Since 2012: Mathematics courses for numerical simulation at ISAE-SUPAERO (first-year Master's): Finite Volume, Finite Element, Finite Difference, Riemann solvers, strong and weak solutions...
  • 2011-2015: CFD courses for engineering students at ISAE-SUPAERO (Master level): Introduction to the Navier-Stokes equations in the continuous regime (triangle of conception, ODE and PDE, hyperbolic...)
  • 2011-2015: CFD courses for engineering students at ISAE-SUPAERO (Master level): Techniques for building a mesh (Cartesian, unstructured, and structured)

Professional training

  • Since 2017: Lecturer in the CERFACS training session on the JAGUAR solver. Course on high-order spectral discontinuous methods.
  • 2014-2017: Organizer of and lecturer in the CERFACS training session entitled Fundamentals to understand and analyze high-fidelity compressible Large Eddy Simulation. Introduction to CFD, meshing techniques. Focus on schemes for LES, including aeroacoustics and shock treatment.
  • 2010: CERFACS e-learning website. Two lessons prepared with H. Deniau for students starting their Ph.D.: Mesh and Discretization of the Navier-Stokes equations and classical convection schemes
  • 2008-2011: CFD courses for engineering students at ISAE-SUPAERO (second year)
  • 2006: Internal course on turbulence for reentry at CEA.

Invited talks for students
